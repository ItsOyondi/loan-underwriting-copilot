Its an Azure native multi-agent copilot for loan underwriting use case. 
Loan case documents needed are: Borrower request, financial statements, Management Report and Credit report.
Its built around LangGraph as the state orchestrate with Azure openai and 7 agents running document intake, (financial assessment, covenant testing and policy retrieval) running in parallel. 
Their combined output is handed off to risk synthesis agent, memo preparation agent(drafting the loan report findings) then finally approval routing agent.
I have exeptionhandler for retries, max-retries and circuit breaker.
It has three human in the loop(after risk synthesis, after memo preparation and after approval routing for final approval).
The system has 5 mcp tools (borrower data, financial cals, covenant testing, workflow status and approval record servers). 
Short term storage is redis and long term storage is pinecone serverless (with two name spaces policy memory and case precedents (previous approved cases).
Observability is done by langsmith and monitoring by Azure monitor(application insights and log analytics). 
For the models is Azure Openai.
