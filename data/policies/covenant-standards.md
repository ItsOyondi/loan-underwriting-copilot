# Covenant standards

Policy ID: POL-COV-01

Standard commercial covenants are a minimum DSCR of 1.25x, a maximum Debt/EBITDA of 3.5x, a minimum current ratio of 1.2x, and a minimum interest coverage of 2.0x. Tests use the latest fiscal year and a pro-forma that includes the new loan. A breach is a policy exception and must be named in the credit memo. Headroom is the distance between the calculated ratio and the covenant level.
