# Commercial lending limits

Policy ID: POL-LIM-01

Secured equipment loans for manufacturing borrowers may be approved up to $5 million when the latest DSCR is at least 1.25x and Debt/EBITDA is at most 3.5x. Requests above $5 million go to the credit committee regardless of the risk grade. Working-capital revolvers for transportation companies require a bureau score of at least 680 and a current ratio of at least 1.2x.
